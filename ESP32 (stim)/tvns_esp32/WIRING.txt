ESP32 DevKit V1 -> tVNS Howland circuit
=======================================

Circuit change from the STM32 version: none. Only the signal sources move.

  ESP32 pin        Goes to                                   Required
  ---------------  ----------------------------------------  --------
  GPIO25 (D25)     Free end of R3 (vin node)                 yes
  GPIO26 (D26)     Free end of R1 (lifted off GND)           yes
  GND              Battery midpoint (circuit GND)            yes
  100k resistor    Across GPIO25 and GPIO26                  recommended
                   (holds current near zero during reset;
                    use 100k, not 10k: ESP32 DAC is weak)
  GPIO27 (D27)     Enable input on the analog side, if any   optional

  Do NOT connect +9 V / -9 V to the ESP32. Power the ESP32 from USB.
  Do NOT power the ESP32 from VIN while USB is plugged in.

Order: USB in and booted -> batteries on -> "on". Shutdown in reverse.

On-board: BOOT button (GPIO0) = emergency stop; blue LED (GPIO2) = stimulating.
Console: Serial Monitor / PuTTY at 115200 on the ESP32's COM port.
Opening the port resets the ESP32 (normal; output starts OFF).

Expected at rest: GPIO25 and GPIO26 both ~1.65 V (code 128).
Expected at 5 mA: GPIO25 steps to ~1.65 +/- 0.50 V (39 codes = ~5.03 mA).
